## A. Group Verification

### 1. What is the biggest issue your team still needs to solve before Week 6?

The team's biggest remaining issue is coordination and shared understanding of the work. Some tasks require specialized knowledge, so members may interpret another member's idea or expected output differently. Work has also been divided mainly by week, although some deliverables require contribution from the whole team. For example, Week 4 focuses on financial logic, but developing that logic is a team process rather than the work of only one assigned member.

### 2. Why is this issue important?

Without a common understanding of each task, members may produce outputs based on different interpretations. This can create inconsistencies between sections and slow the project because the team must review and revise the work together later.

### 3. What has your team done about this issue so far?

After identifying the issue, the team started holding more regular progress updates. Members also cross-check sections prepared by others, discuss overlaps, and resolve inconsistencies before treating a deliverable as complete.

### 4. What will your team do next about this issue?

The team will communicate task expectations more clearly and confirm the intended output before starting each deliverable. Important terms and logic will be explained consistently so that the tester, UI/UX designer, and developer share the same understanding. Regular updates and cross-checks will continue throughout implementation.
